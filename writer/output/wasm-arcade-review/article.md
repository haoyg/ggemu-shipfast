# WASM Arcade Review: Games, Compatibility, and Alternatives

WASM Arcade is a third-party browser-game catalog that combines PC engine ports or reimplementations, arcade emulation, and Flash playback. This independent review explains what the site currently lists, how its games run, where saves are kept, and what to check before opening a game.

## What is WASM Arcade?

WASM Arcade is a website that groups several kinds of browser-playable games under one catalog. Its name refers to WebAssembly, a browser technology that can run compiled code inside a web page. The official documentation says the collection includes native or reimplemented game engines, arcade emulation, and Flash games running through Ruffle.

The games do not all use the same technology. The PC section names projects such as re3, reVC, and EaglercraftX. Arcade titles use EmulatorJS with FinalBurn Neo, while Flash games run through Ruffle. “WASM Arcade” is therefore the site’s name, not one universal emulator.

## Which games are listed on WASM Arcade?

The official catalog reported 1,082 entries when checked on October 13, 2026. Its five public collections were PC games, Neo Geo, Capcom arcade, arcade classics, and Flash games.

These figures are a dated snapshot. Different official pages briefly showed different totals during review, and individual builds can be added, removed, or reclassified. Check the official catalog for current availability.

## Browser compatibility and device requirements

The PC collection recommends current Chrome, Edge, or Firefox on a desktop or laptop, using keyboard and mouse or a gamepad. The about page also mentions Safari 15.4 or later for general WebAssembly and WebGL 2 support, while still presenting desktop Chrome, Edge, and Firefox as the better fit for its GTA builds.

Browser support alone does not predict performance. CPU speed, graphics drivers, available memory, device temperature, extensions, cache state, and game-data size all matter. POKOPIE has not independently benchmarked these builds, so this review does not publish frame-rate or loading-speed scores.

## Loading, caching, and local saves

WASM Arcade says game data can stream and cache in the browser. A first visit may therefore wait for more data than a return visit. There is no single reliable loading-time figure because the result depends on the game, connection, server conditions, cache, and device.

Its privacy and about pages state that saves use browser storage and that some game pages provide a Save Manager. Clearing site data, changing browser profiles, or moving to another device can remove access to local progress. Export important saves when the game provides that option.

## Advertising and privacy trade-offs

The site says no account is required. Its privacy policy identifies Google AdSense and Monetag as advertising partners. It also says pressing Play may open a sponsor page in another tab, while browsing pages may open an advertising tab after a click.

Keep browser pop-up controls enabled and close unexpected sponsor tabs rather than entering information into them. POKOPIE cannot verify or control advertising displayed by a separate website.

## Ownership, ports, and availability

WASM Arcade describes itself as unofficial, says it does not own the listed games, and publishes a copyright and takedown process. Open-source engine code does not automatically make original artwork, audio, maps, or other game data freely redistributable.

Browser availability does not settle ownership or licensing questions. Follow the requirements shown for each build, use files only when you have the right to use them, and expect availability to change.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is WASM Arcade part of POKOPIE?

No. It is a separate third-party website. POKOPIE does not operate it, host its catalog, or control its availability and advertising.

### Does WASM Arcade require a download?

The site presents its games as browser-playable without a traditional installer. Game data still transfers to the browser and may be cached locally, so “no download” does not mean zero network data or storage use.

### Can I play on mobile?

Support varies. The official site recommends desktop Chrome, Edge, or Firefox for its PC collection, although it says some GTA builds include touch controls.

### Where are saves stored?

The site says saves are stored in the browser, primarily through IndexedDB, with local storage used for some settings. Clearing site data can remove them.

### Has POKOPIE tested every game?

No. This review covers the public catalog and official documentation as of October 13, 2026. It does not claim independent performance testing of every build.

## Conclusion

WASM Arcade is useful as a single directory for several browser-game technologies, but its catalog and individual builds can change. Check the current official listing, use a modern desktop browser for demanding games, back up local saves, and review the advertising and rights information before relying on a specific title.
