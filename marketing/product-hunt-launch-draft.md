## Maker first comment

> Hi Product Hunt — I’m Hugh, the developer of POKOPIE. I built its browser compatibility check because "this emulator works in a browser" does not tell you whether a particular browser has the APIs it needs, or whether the device will perform well.
>
> The check runs locally and looks at WebAssembly, WebGL 2, IndexedDB, gamepad, fullscreen, and shared-memory support. It explains why each capability matters, and it is deliberately not presented as a performance benchmark: supported APIs alone cannot predict how every game will run.
>
> I would value feedback on the methodology and on any browser capability you think should be tested or explained differently.
