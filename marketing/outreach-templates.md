# POKOPIE Outreach Kit

## Approved positioning

**One line**

POKOPIE is a browser-based retro gaming platform with a compatibility checker and a player for ROM files that users are legally permitted to use.

**Short description**

POKOPIE helps players check whether their browser supports the capabilities commonly used by PS1 emulators, explore retro game collections, and load their own legally obtained ROM files without installing a desktop emulator.

## Community feedback post

**Title:** I built a local browser capability check for web-based PS1 emulation

I maintain POKOPIE and wanted a clearer way to distinguish browser API support from actual emulator performance. I built a small checker for WebAssembly, WebGL 2, IndexedDB, gamepad, fullscreen, and shared-memory availability.

The test runs entirely in the browser and does not upload device details or ROM files. I have also documented the weighting and limitations rather than presenting the score as a performance benchmark.

I would appreciate technical feedback on the methodology, especially whether any capability should be weighted differently:

https://pokopie.com/en/ps1-compatibility

Disclosure: I am the developer of the linked site.

## Operating rules

1. Personalize every editorial or resource-page message with one specific reference.
2. Send no more than ten new pitches per week during the first month.
3. Follow up once after seven days, then close the prospect.
4. Never request keyword-rich anchor text.
5. Do not buy links that pass ranking credit or use automated submission services.
6. Disclose ownership in community posts and follow local self-promotion rules.
7. Mark paid placements with `rel="sponsored"`; accept `nofollow` or `ugc` links without negotiation.
8. Record the live URL and referral traffic after every successful placement.
